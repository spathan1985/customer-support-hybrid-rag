## Overview
The EchoLight Bulb B500 is a fictional smart bulb for home use. It connects through the AsterHome companion app.

## Setup
1. Connect the device to power.
2. Open the AsterHome app and choose Add Device.
3. Pair using a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
4. Confirm the device appears online.

## Operation
Use the companion app to view status, change preferences, and check firmware. Keep the device away from water and excessive heat.

## Error codes
### E512: device unreachable
Meaning: device unreachable. Recommended action: toggle the wall switch on, power cycle the bulb, and reconnect through the app.

## Known symptom
### brightness changes unexpectedly
Recommended action: disable adaptive brightness and review saved automations.

## Reset and maintenance
Restart by removing power for 15 seconds. For network reset, select Device Settings > Network > Reset Network. Inspect the device monthly for visible damage.

## Warranty and escalation
Fictional limited warranty: 12 months from purchase. If troubleshooting fails, provide model, firmware version, symptoms, and error code to support. Do not disassemble the device.
